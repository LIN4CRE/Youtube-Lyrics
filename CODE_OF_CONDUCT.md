# Community standards

Youtube-Lyrics welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, constructive, and specific when discussing code, design, and reports.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith and focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with contributors and maintainers.
- Respect privacy: do not share another person's logs, track history, or personal details without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These standards apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately through the [LIN4CRE GitHub profile](https://github.com/LIN4CRE). Please do not use a public issue for sensitive details.

The maintainer may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community. The project is volunteer-maintained and does not promise a formal response-time SLA.
